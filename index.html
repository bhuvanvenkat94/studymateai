A few quick answers

FAQ

What is StudyMate AI?

StudyMate AI is a concept for an AI-powered study companion that helps learners understand topics, summarize notes, practice, and plan study sessions.

Can it replace teachers or textbooks?

No. It is designed as a learning aid. Use your teachers, textbooks, and trusted resources, and verify important information.

Is the demo connected to a real AI?

No. This website is a front-end prototype. The demo creates a prompt for you to copy; it does not generate AI answers or store your input.

Is StudyMate AI available to use?

This is a fictional startup concept and demonstration website created for a student project, not a released product.
